Frequently asked questions

What do I need to use the app?

A CDD Vault account and an API token for your vault. You add the vault in the app (EU or US server), and the app syncs its inventory to your phone.

Why can't I deplete, move or edit samples?

Every vault is read-only by default. Writes are enabled only when you mark the vault's token as read/write in the vault settings, and the token itself must have write permission in CDD Vault.

A sample is missing or out of date in the app.

Run a sync. The app shows only what was synced from CDD Vault; changes made in CDD after the last sync appear after the next one.

Does the app work without a signal?

Yes. Browsing, searching and checking sessions work offline from the synced data. Writes to CDD Vault and syncing need a connection.

Where is my API token stored?

Only on your device, encrypted by the system (Keychain on iOS, Keystore on Android). It is sent only to the CDD Vault server you configured. See the privacy policy.

Does the app collect any data?

The developer receives no data — the app has no server of its own. The iOS app talks only to your CDD Vault server. On Android, the scanner (Google ML Kit) also sends Google technical diagnostics such as device model, app version and error codes; never camera images, scanned codes, your token or inventory data. Details are in the privacy policy.

Is this an official CDD Vault app?

No. CDD Inventory is an independent app and is not affiliated with Collaborative Drug Discovery, Inc.
